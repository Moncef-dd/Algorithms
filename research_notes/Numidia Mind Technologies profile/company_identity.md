**Read this first — access limits that shape every finding below**

- This research session could not open the company's own websites, LinkedIn, Hugging Face, the Wayback Machine or RDAP/WHOIS. Direct fetches of `www.numidiamind.com`, `numidiamind.com`, `www.numidiatec.com`, `numidiatec.com`, `www.linkedin.com` and `huggingface.co` failed with a host-resolution error that this environment returns for any host outside its network allowlist (a control lookup of `example.com` failed the same way, so the failure says nothing about whether the domains exist). `web.archive.org` was refused by the fetch tool and `rdap.org` was blocked by the proxy (HTTP 403).

Treat these as snippet-level evidence (undated; the index does not show a capture date).
- Net result: the company has almost no indexed third-party footprint. No registry entry, press article, directory listing, LinkedIn page, job post, funding record or named person was found. Most key questions end in "not found".

Evidence grading used below: **[V]** verified by direct page read; **[S]** search-snippet level only (single source, not read directly); **[N]** negative result (searched, nothing found).
