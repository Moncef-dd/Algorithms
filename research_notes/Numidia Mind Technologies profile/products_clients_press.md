- TeamPilot: no public mention tied to NMT. - The German Teampilot AI is the namesake most likely to be confused with NMT's TeamPilot, since both are enterprise AI workspaces. Whether NMT's product reuses or relates to it is unknown; nothing suggests it does.
- No measured results (accuracy, false-positive rate, latency, benchmark) exist publicly for any product, so any performance figure would have to come from the vendor directly.
