# Open questions

Things the catalogue does not answer. Each needs a human answer before it can become a fact in `docs/` or
`products/`. Move resolved items to the relevant file and delete them here.

## Company

- [ ] Founding date, legal form, ownership, headcount, team and skills.
- [ ] Is "NMT" short for Numidia Mind Technologies? Any other brand names or entities?
- [ ] Funding, revenue, runway. Is this a venture, a services firm that productised, or a subsidiary?
- [ ] Geographic scope: Algeria only, wider Maghreb/Africa, France/Europe, Gulf?
- [ ] Certifications and compliance status (the catalogue says "conformité" repeatedly but names no standard).

## Market and customers

- [ ] Existing customers, pilots, references. Any that can be named publicly?
- [ ] Which regulators and rules apply (Bank of Algeria, anti-money-laundering rules, national data
      protection law)? Which does each product map to?
- [ ] Main competitors per product and the reasons customers pick or reject NMT.
- [ ] Sales cycle, buyer and budget owner per product.

## Commercial

- [ ] Pricing and commercial model per product.
- [ ] Delivery model: SaaS, on-premise, hybrid? Who operates it after go-live?
- [ ] What "sur mesure" means in practice: configuration, or custom development per client?

## Product and technology (per product detail lives in `products/*/README.md`)

- [ ] Status of each product, in plain terms: production with customers, ready to deploy, prototype.
- [ ] Models and stack behind each product: own models or third-party; which languages and document types
      (Arabic, French, Tamazight) for SDR; training data origin for Shield/KYC 360.
- [ ] Measured performance (detection rate, false-positive rate, OCR accuracy, latency).
- [ ] Which products are integrated with each other, and which share a platform.
- [ ] Where the code lives and who maintains it.
